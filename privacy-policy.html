2. Tastatureingaben

Typevo ist eine Bildschirmtastatur. Alle Eingaben werden ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät verarbeitet – Vorschläge, Autokorrektur, Wischtippen, Korrekturlesen und Übersetzen. Gelernte Wörter, Tipp-Statistik, angepasste Tastenbereiche, Textbausteine und – wenn eingeschaltet – der Verlauf der Zwischenablage werden nur lokal in der App gespeichert. In Passwortfeldern und im Inkognito-Modus lernt und speichert Typevo nichts. Typevo überträgt deine Tastatureingaben weder an uns noch an Dritte.

4. Support und Wünsche

Schickst du über „Persönlicher Support“ oder „Wunsch äußern“ eine Nachricht, öffnet Typevo deine eigene App (z. B. E-Mail). Übertragen wird nur, was du absendest: dein Text, die App-Version, die Android-Version und das Handymodell sowie – wenn du es auswählst – ein Link zu deiner aktuellen Tastatur. Wir nutzen diese Daten nur, um zu antworten bzw. deinen Wunsch umzusetzen (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b und f DSGVO), und löschen sie, sobald die Anfrage erledigt ist – spätestens nach 12 Monaten, sofern keine gesetzliche Aufbewahrungspflicht besteht. Für den Versand gelten die Bedingungen deines E-Mail- bzw. Messenger-Anbieters.

5. Fehlerberichte und Sicherung

Der Fehler-Melder speichert Abstürze und Fehler nur auf deinem Gerät. Ein Bericht verlässt das Gerät nur, wenn du ihn selbst kopierst oder teilst. Die Funktion „Sicherung exportieren“ speichert eine Datei an einem Ort, den du auswählst; der API-Schlüssel ist nie enthalten, die Zwischenablage nur auf Wunsch.

6. Berechtigungen der App

  • Internet – für Schriften, Sprachpakete und die optionale Gemini-API
  • Benachrichtigungen – für Erinnerungen und den Fokus-Timer (optional)
  • Vibration – für haptisches Feedback beim Tippen

Typevo verlangt keinen Zugriff auf Kontakte, Standort, Mikrofon, Kamera oder deine Dateien. Bilder für Tasten oder Hintergründe wählst du selbst über die Bildauswahl von Android.

In short

  • What you type stays on your device. We never see your input.
  • Data only leaves the device for features you trigger yourself (e.g. downloading a font, your own Gemini key, a support message).
  • The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from third-party servers.

2. Keyboard input

All typing is processed on your device only – predictions, autocorrect, glide typing, proofreading and translation. Learned words, typing statistics, touch adaptation, text snippets and – if enabled – clipboard history are stored locally in the app. Nothing is learned or stored in password fields or in incognito mode. Typevo never sends your keystrokes to us or to third parties.

3. When data leaves the device

  • Google fonts (if you pick one): the font file is downloaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address. No text is sent. (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • On-device AI and translation: Gemini Nano (Android AICore) and language packs (ML Kit) are downloaded from Google on request; your text is then processed on the device. (Art. 6(1)(b))
  • Your own Gemini API key (optional): only if you enter a key and tap an AI action, the selected text is sent over HTTPS to Google's Gemini API under Google's terms. The key stays on the device and is never backed up or exported. (Art. 6(1)(b))
  • “Ask Gemini” hands the text to the Gemini app / assistant on your device.
  • Android backup: if Google backup is on, Android may back up settings and designs to your Google account (never the API key).

Typevo has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking tools.

4. Support and wishes

When you send a message via “Personal support” or “Make a wish”, Typevo opens your own app (e.g. e-mail). Only what you send is transferred: your text, the app version, Android version and phone model and – if you choose – a link to your current keyboard. We only use it to answer or to build your wish (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete it once the request is done – at the latest after 12 months unless the law requires us to keep it.

5. Error reports and backups

The error detector stores crashes and errors on the device only; a report leaves the device only if you copy or share it yourself. “Export backup” saves a file where you choose; it never contains the API key, and the clipboard only if you want.

6. App permissions

Internet (fonts, language packs, optional Gemini API), notifications (optional reminders and focus timer), vibration (haptic feedback). No access to contacts, location, microphone, camera or your files.

7. This website

Hosting: the website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., San Francisco, USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as IP address, date and time, requested page and browser type in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA; GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See GitHub's privacy statement.

No cookies, no tracking: no cookies, analytics or ads. Fonts, images, videos and animations are served from the same server – nothing is loaded from third parties. Your language choice is only remembered in your browser's local storage.

8. Links to other services

The website and the app link to Google Play, Telegram and Discord. Their privacy policies only apply once you click a link; no data is sent to them before.

9. Children

Typevo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR) – just write to the e-mail address above – and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. As Typevo does not send your input to us, we usually hold no data about you apart from messages you sent us. Delete all data on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings.

11. Changes

If the app or website changes in a way that requires an update, the new version will be published here with a new date.
